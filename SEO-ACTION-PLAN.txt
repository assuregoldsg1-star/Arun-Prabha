------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 1 - GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE  (DO THIS TODAY - critical)
------------------------------------------------------------
  Without this, Google may take MONTHS to find you. With it,
  days.

  1. Go to search.google.com/search-console
  2. Sign in with a Google account
  3. Add property -> Domain -> type: assuregold.sg
  4. Google gives you a TXT record. Add it in CLDY:
       - CLDY -> Edit DNS -> New Record
       - Type: TXT, Name: blank/@, Value: (paste Google's code)
       - Save
  5. Back in Search Console -> Verify
  6. Left menu -> Sitemaps -> enter: sitemap.xml -> Submit
  7. Top search bar -> paste https://assuregold.sg/ ->
     Enter -> "Request Indexing"
  8. Repeat step 7 for:
       https://assuregold.sg/collection.html
       https://assuregold.sg/buyback.html
       https://assuregold.sg/newchains.html
