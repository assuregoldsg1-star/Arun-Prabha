Science of Trust

Precision Purity Testing

We use advanced XRF (X-ray fluorescence) analyser technology — the same equipment used by government assay offices — to deliver definitive, non-destructive purity results in seconds.

XRF Analysis

High-end X-ray fluorescence analysis gives elemental composition to 4 decimal places — accurate to 0.01% purity with no surface damage.

Live Before Purchase

You watch the test happen in front of you — results on screen within 30 seconds. No hidden steps, no delays. You see exactly what you are buying.

Certified Results

Every piece receives a documented purity certification — a record of its gold content and assay results that you keep with your purchase.

Non-Destructive

XRF testing leaves your ornament completely intact and unharmed. The X-ray beam reads the surface — there is no drilling, scratching, or acid use.
