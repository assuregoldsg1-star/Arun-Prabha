5. XRF purity testing

We use professional XRF (X-Ray Fluorescence) spectrometry equipment to test gold purity. This is the same technology used by government assay offices and major gold dealers. Results are reported to four decimal places (e.g. 91.6431%).

The XRF test is non-destructive — it does not damage your item in any way. You are welcome to watch the reading in real time. We share the result with you before any transaction is finalised.
