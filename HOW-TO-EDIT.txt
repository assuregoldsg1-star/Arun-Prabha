==================================================================
   ASSURE GOLD — HOW TO EDIT YOUR WEBSITE
   A simple guide. No coding. No technical setup.
==================================================================

You edit your website using the admin tool on your computer,
then put the updated files into GitHub. Your live website
updates by itself.

------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 1 — OPEN THE ADMIN TOOL
------------------------------------------------------------

  Find the file:   ag-admin.html
  Double-click it. It opens in your web browser.
  This is your editing panel.

------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 2 — MAKE YOUR CHANGES
------------------------------------------------------------

  Use the menu on the left. You can edit:

    Products & Prices  - any price, add or remove products,
                         names, weights, descriptions
    Featured on Home   - the 3 products shown on the homepage
    Photos & Images    - replace any product photo, carousel
                         image, or the logo (you see a preview
                         of the current photo)
    Hero Carousel      - the big rotating banners on top
    Website Text       - section headings and the stats bar
    Business Info      - WhatsApp number, address, hours

  Make as many changes as you like. They are saved in the
  tool as you go.

------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 3 — GET YOUR UPDATED FILES
------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Click  "Publish"  in the left menu.

  2. Click the file box and select your 3 website files:
        index.html
        collection.html
        buyback.html
     (They are in this same folder. Hold the Ctrl key to
      click and select all 3 together.)

  3. Click  "Build & Download ZIP".

  4. A ZIP file downloads to your computer. It has all your
     changes inside, ready to use.

------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 4 — PUT THE FILES INTO GITHUB
------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Unzip the downloaded file (right-click -> Extract).

  2. Go to github.com and open your website repository.

  3. Click  "Add file"  ->  "Upload files".

  4. Drag the updated files from the unzipped folder into
     the page.

  5. Click  "Commit changes" at the bottom.

  That is it. Your website updates by itself in about
  half a minute. Open your website and refresh the page
  to see the changes.

------------------------------------------------------------
  GOOD TO KNOW
------------------------------------------------------------

  - BACKUP: In the Publish section, click "Download Backup"
    to save a copy of all your content. Keep it safe. If
    something ever goes wrong, "Restore Backup" brings it
    all back.

  - PHOTOS: Square photos look best for products. When you
    change a photo, it is included in the ZIP automatically.

  - KEEP THE FOLDER TOGETHER: Do not move the "assets"
    folder or the images will stop showing.
